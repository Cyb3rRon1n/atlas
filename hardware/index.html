    Supported hardware¶

    Lanxang works on any home network with no logins at all: it finds every device from its own LAN scan, watches for rogue DHCP servers and spoofed gateways, lists open ports, checks your public certificates, and runs uptime checks. Connecting your gear adds detail - switch ports, Wi-Fi access points, VMs. Everything lanxang reads is read-only, and it never changes a device's settings.

    On the Setup tab, What's on your network? asks what you have and shows only the matching cards. Gear lanxang has already found is ticked for you.

    A TrueNAS mirror losing a disk, as lanxang shows it.

    SNMPv3 or v2c? Use v3 whenever the device offers it (a read-only user, SHA + AES). Many cheaper switches, routers and NAS boxes only speak v2c: lanxang reads them with a read-only community, which travels unencrypted, so give it no write access. Unmanaged switches and "Easy Smart" / "Plus" switches with no SNMP can't show their ports - lanxang still sees every device behind them, and lists them on the port of the managed switch they hang off.

    Not supported: mesh systems and routers that are only managed from a phone app or vendor cloud (eero, Google/Nest Wifi, Deco, Orbi, Firewalla) have no local interface lanxang can read. Your devices still show up from the LAN scan.

    Coming next¶

    From our research into what homes and home labs actually run, roughly in this order:

    1. Router details - DHCP leases and Wi-Fi clients from OPNsense, OpenWrt, MikroTik and Fritz!Box (UniFi has it already).
    2. TP-Link Omada and Unraid, and APC network management cards read over SNMP (for a UPS with no NUT server).
